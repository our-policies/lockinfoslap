Lockfy Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 September 2026 · App: Lockfy (Android package com.abdullahwins.LockInfoSlap) · Developer: NADESOFT

In short: Lockfy does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. Everything you create is made and kept on your own device. The app has no account, no ads, no analytics and does not send your information over the internet.

1. Who we are

Lockfy is a lock screen wallpaper maker published by NADESOFT ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the app handles information. If you have questions, contact us at contact@nadesoft.bd.

2. Information you enter in the app

To build a wallpaper you may type details such as a name, emergency contact phone numbers, medical information (for example blood type, allergies or conditions), or "if found" return details.

  • This text is used only to draw your wallpaper on your device.
  • Your current design is saved in the app's private storage on your device so it is still there next time you open the app.
  • It is never sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it.
  • You can delete it at any time with Layers → Clear canvas, by clearing the app's data in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Anything you place on your lock screen is visible to anyone who looks at your phone. Please include only what a helper truly needs, and avoid full addresses, ID numbers or other sensitive details.

3. Photos

  • If you choose to use a photo as your background, you pick it with Android's system photo picker. Lockfy only receives the single photo you select, and does not get access to the rest of your photo library.
  • The photo is processed on your device only. It is not uploaded anywhere.

4. Saving and setting your wallpaper

  • When you tap save, Lockfy writes the finished wallpaper image to your device's photo library. This is the only reason the app asks for permission to add photos.
  • When you tap Set as wallpaper, the image is handed to Android's own wallpaper chooser on your device.

5. Data we collect or share

None. Lockfy:

  • does not collect personal or device information;
  • does not include advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software;
  • does not share or sell data to third parties;
  • does not require an account or sign-in;
  • does not use your location, camera, microphone or contacts.

6. Purchases

Lockfy is purchased through Google Play. Payment is processed entirely by Google. We never receive your payment card details. Google's handling of that information is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

7. Children

Lockfy is not directed at children under 13, and because the app collects no personal data, it does not knowingly collect data from children. A parent may use Lockfy to make a safety wallpaper for a child's device. That information stays on the device, as described above.

8. Security and retention

Your designs and settings stay in the app's private storage on your device until you clear them or uninstall the app. We hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to retain, disclose or delete on your behalf.

9. Your rights

Because we do not collect or hold personal data about you, there is no personal data for us to access, correct, export or erase. You stay in full control of everything on your device. You can still contact us with any privacy question.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. If a change affects how your information is handled, we will also mention it in the app's release notes.
