Privacy Policy

Effective Date: December 6, 2025

1. Information Collection

LockInfoSlap is designed to work entirely on your device. We do not collect or transmit any personal information unless you explicitly choose to share it.

  • Photos / Media Library: To let you select or save wallpapers.
  • Device Storage: To save wallpapers you create.

All data remains on your device. We do not store your photos or contact information on our servers.

2. How Information is Used

Any access requested is only to enable app functionality:

  • Select a photo or wallpaper
  • Add text, contact info, or emergency info to wallpapers
  • Save the customized lockscreen wallpaper

We never sell or share your information with third parties.

3. Permissions

  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / Photo Library Access: To select photos.
  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android): To save wallpapers.

You can revoke permissions at any time in your device settings.

4. Children’s Privacy

LockInfoSlap is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Security

All edits and saved wallpapers stay on your device. No personal information is transmitted over the internet.
